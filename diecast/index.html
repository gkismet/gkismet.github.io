Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding orders, shipping methods, and model specifications.

Are the models sold by Diecast Models Wholesale authentic?
Yes, 100%. Diecast Models Wholesale is an authorized direct distributor for world-renowned model manufacturers including AutoArt, GT Spirit, Norev, Greenlight, Maisto, Sunstar, and First Gear. Every model comes licensed with genuine manufacturer hallmarks.
How does Diecast Models Wholesale prevent damage to fragile model parts?
Collector models feature delicate wipers, wing mirrors, and spoilers. Diecast Models Wholesale packs every order using dedicated shock-resistant double boxing and heavy-duty bubble encasement, ensuring packages survive postal carrier handling unscathed.
What is the difference between Diecast (Metal) and Composite/Resin models?
Traditional diecast uses zinc alloy (Zamak) which gives the car a substantial, heavy feel with opening doors and hoods. Resin and composite models (such as GT Spirit or OttOmobile) are crafted for razor-sharp body panel lines and flawless paintwork, usually produced as sealed bodyshells without opening parts.
Do they offer international shipping outside the United States?
Yes. In addition to fast domestic continental US shipping, Diecast Models Wholesale ships worldwide via USPS Priority International and FedEx to collectors across Canada, the UK, Australia, Europe, and Asia.
