Our Core Mission

CyberGuard Labs was founded in 2024 by an independent consortium of network reliability engineers and digital security researchers. In an online environment flooded with opaque claims and recycled marketing copy, our singular goal is to provide consumers and small business owners with transparent, reproducible, data-driven security software ratings.

Our Testing Principles

Unlike standard marketing comparison aggregators, we purchase retail subscriptions to every single software package we evaluate. We do not accept sponsored placements, sponsored rating adjustments, or pre-written corporate reviews.

  • Standardized Hardware: All network speed benchmarks are executed on dedicated hardware environments running optical gigabit connections under controlled parameters.
  • Zero-Logs & DNS Leak Scrutiny: We simulate real-world packet drops and sudden interface disconnects to confirm kill-switch reliability and DNS security.
  • Longitudinal Observation: A service must maintain reliable uptime and consistent throughput over a minimum 7-day observation trial before appearing on our leaderboards.

Editorial Independence

CyberGuard Labs is completely reader-supported. While we may earn referral commissions through transparent affiliate relationships when visitors subscribe to software through our links, our testing laboratory operates behind a strict firewall from affiliate partnerships.
