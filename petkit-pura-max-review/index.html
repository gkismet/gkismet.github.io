The Verdict on Safety: In our 90 days of continuous operation, the PURA MAX triggered safety stops 100% of the time whenever either cat poked its head or stepped onto the entrance step.

PETKIT engineered this machine with their proprietary xSecure System, which combines 11 high-precision components:

  • Thermal / Infrared Motion Sensors: Detects approaching pets up to 8 inches away and freezes the drum instantly.
  • 4 High-Precision Weight Sensors: Continuously calibrated at the base. If a cat weighing even 1.5 lbs steps onto the rim, the cycle cancels.

2. Odor Control: Does Your House Still Smell?

A major flaw of conventional litter boxes is that waste sits exposed to open air. The PURA MAX tackles odor with a three-stage suppression system:

  1. Hermetically Sealed Waste Chute: When the cylinder is in neutral position, the waste drawer opening is sealed with silicone gaskets to prevent stink from wafting back up.
  2. Pura Air Smart Spray Module: An automated misting module clips to the interior wall and dispenses an antibacterial, odor-neutralizing mist after every visit.
  3. Automated Ventilation Schedule: You can configure the PETKIT companion app to run an extra deodorizing spray cycle every 2 hours if you have multiple cats.

In our living room test, guests could not smell the litter box even when sitting 4 feet away.

4. Cleaning & Everyday Maintenance

An automated litter box is useless if taking it apart to clean takes 45 minutes of scrub time. Here is what daily maintenance looks like:

  • Every 10-14 days: Pull out the bottom waste drawer, pull the drawstring on the garbage liner bag, tie it off, toss it in the trash, and slide in a fresh liner. Total time: 45 seconds.
  • Every 4-6 weeks: Lift off the rotating cylinder (it detaches with no screws or tools required), carry it outside or to the bathtub, hose it down with water, and let it dry. Total time: 10 minutes.

5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Does PURA MAX require special expensive litter?

No! That is one of its best perks. You can use standard clumping clay litter, tofu litter, cassava litter, or mixed pellets. Avoid non-clumping or paper pellet litter.

Q: Can kittens use the PURA MAX?

Kittens under 3.5 lbs (usually under 6 months old) should NOT use the automated cycling mode because their weight is too light to reliably trigger standard weight sensors. You can keep it in manual mode until they grow to 4 lbs.

Q: What is included in the official retail box?

The standard package includes the PURA MAX unit, magnetic power adapter, 1 roll of custom waste trash bags, 1 bottle of smart spray purification liquid, and user guide.
