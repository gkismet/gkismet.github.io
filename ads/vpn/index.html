How We Test & Benchmark VPNs

Our evaluation methodology is purely data-driven. We install each VPN client on clean macOS, Windows 11, iOS, and Android testbeds connected to dedicated 1Gbps optical fiber networks. Every VPN is tested across 3 distinct times of day for 7 consecutive days to assess speed consistency, ping fluctuation, and kill switch reliability during intentional disconnections.
