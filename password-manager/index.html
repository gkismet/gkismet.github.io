Frequently Asked Questions About Password Managers

What makes XChaCha20 encryption superior to standard AES-256?

While AES-256 is strong, it requires hardware acceleration to run efficiently. XChaCha20 is a modern stream cipher adopted by tech leaders like Google and Cloudflare because it does not require hardware acceleration, eliminates cache-timing attacks, and provides faster cryptographic operations with virtually impossible key collisions.

Can NordPass employees or hackers see my master password or vault?

No. NordPass operates on a strictly enforced Zero-Knowledge architecture. Your master password and vault data are encrypted locally on your device before being synced to the cloud. Only you hold the decryption keys—neither NordPass engineers, government bodies, nor hackers can ever access your passwords.

How easy is it to import my existing passwords from Chrome, Apple, or 1Password?

Extremely easy. NordPass features an automatic 1-click import wizard that directly ingests CSV files from Google Chrome, Apple iCloud Keychain, Firefox, LastPass, Bitwarden, and 1Password in under 60 seconds.
