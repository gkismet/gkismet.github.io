Frequently Asked Questions About Replacement Parts

How do I accurately measure my trampoline to avoid ordering the wrong size?

Measure across the outer metal edge of your frame in at least two places to ensure it is round. Next, count the exact number of triangular V-rings or spring holes. Finally, unhook one spring and measure its total relaxed length from outer hook tip to outer hook tip. With frame size, spring count, and spring length, fitment is guaranteed.

Will these parts fit my Skywalker, JumpSport, or Upper Bounce trampoline?

Yes. Trampoline Parts and Supply manufactures components designed to exact OEM specifications, fitting all major backyard trampoline brands sold in the United States over the last 20 years.

When should trampoline springs be replaced?

Inspect your springs annually. If any coils have stretched gaps where daylight shows through when unweighted, or if the spring recoil feels spongy, the steel has fatigued. Replacing tired springs restores full bounce momentum and prevents frame warping.

What is the warranty and shipping timeframe?

Jumping beds carry up to a 5-year manufacturer pro-rated warranty against stitching failure. All parts ship directly from US logistics centers and arrive at most US residential addresses in 2 to 5 business days.
