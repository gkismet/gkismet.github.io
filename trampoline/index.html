How to Order the Exact Correct Size (3-Step Fitment Guarantee)

Don't worry about ordering the wrong part. Follow these 3 simple measurements before clicking through:

1

Measure Frame Diameter

Measure from outside metal frame edge to outside metal frame edge across the middle of your trampoline.

2

Count Spring Holes

Count the total number of spring holes or V-rings around the entire circumference of your mat.

3

Measure Spring Length

Remove one unstretched spring and measure from the tip of hook to the tip of opposite hook.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will these parts fit my Skywalker trampoline?

Yes. Trampoline Parts and Supply manufactures universal precision-fit components engineered to fit all major round and rectangle brands including Skywalker, JumpSport, JumpKing, and Upper Bounce.

Where do parts ship from?

All replacement parts ship directly from centralized warehouses within the United States. Domestic orders are typically dispatched within 24 hours with reliable ground delivery.

What warranty is included on jumping mats?

All Grade-A Permatron jumping mats include a multi-year manufacturer warranty protecting against seam separation, V-ring pullout, and accelerated UV degradation.

How do I know if my springs need replacement?

If your springs show visible gaps in the coils while unstretched, rust corrosion, or your trampoline feels saggy during bounce, replacing the springs restores like-new lift.
