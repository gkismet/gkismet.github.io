Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 10, 2026

At CyberGuard Labs ("we", "our", or "us"), accessible from cyberguard-labs.org, protecting the personal privacy of our website visitors is one of our primary core values. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information gathered and recorded by CyberGuard Labs and how we utilize it in accordance with applicable global data privacy regulations (including GDPR and CCPA).

1. Information We Collect

CyberGuard Labs does not require users to create personal accounts, register personal profile data, or provide credit card information to access our laboratory benchmark reports and editorial reviews.

We may automatically collect standard non-personally identifiable technical information through automated logs, including:

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system specification
  • General aggregated geographic region (country/city level)
  • Referring and exit pages, timestamps, and visit duration

2. Cookies and Web Analytics

Like any modern digital publisher, CyberGuard Labs uses standard analytical cookies to record visitor interaction data, evaluate page loading performance, and improve our benchmarking guides. You may disable cookies at any time through your specific browser settings.

3. Third-Party Links & Affiliate Disclosure

Our website contains direct outbound hyperlinks to external commercial service providers (such as VPN providers). If you click on an external link and make a purchase on a provider's official portal, we may receive an affiliate referral commission. We do not transmit any personal data or personally identifiable information to these merchants upon clicking.

4. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

Under CCPA, California consumers maintain the right to:

  • Request disclosure of the specific categories of personal data collected.
  • Request the deletion of any collected consumer personal data.
  • Opt-out of any prospective sale of personal data (CyberGuard Labs does NOT sell personal user data under any circumstances).

5. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

Every European Economic Area (EEA) resident is entitled to access, rectify, restrict processing, or request data portability concerning their personal records. For inquiries, please reach out via our contact channels.
