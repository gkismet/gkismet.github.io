Multi-Cat Owners Frequently Ask

Will skittish or nervous cats be afraid of the PURA MAX? +
In our tests with two timid shelter rescues, both transitioned within 48 hours. Because the PURA MAX emits only 34.8 dB (comparable to a quiet refrigerator hum) and sits just 7.8 inches off the ground, cats do not feel intimidated like they often do with the tall, cave-like Litter-Robot 4.
Does urine ever leak between the drum and the base? +
No. The PURA MAX drum features a seamless TPE cylinder lining with raised walls. Even high-peeing cats cannot spray outside the drum or between mechanical gears.
Can I use standard trash bags instead of official refills? +
Yes! While PETKIT's official drawstring liner bags fit precisely into the silicone rim fasteners, standard 4-gallon small trash can liners work completely fine, saving you even more money over time.
How often do I need to empty the waste drawer? +
For a single adult cat, the 7-liter waste compartment easily lasts 12 to 15 days before needing an empty. For 2 cats, you will typically change the bag once every 6 to 7 days.
