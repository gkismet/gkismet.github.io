Editorial Guidelines & Password Security Standards

Our Benchmark Methodology, Ethics Code, and Rating Architecture
Our Editorial Pledge: Vendor partnerships and affiliate arrangements never influence which password managers are tested, which products earn an award badge, or how software is scored on our benchmark metrics.

1. The Four-Pillar Security & Usability Metric (10-Point Scale)

Every password vault evaluated by CyberGuard Labs is rated across four core weighted dimensions:

  • Cryptographic Resilience & Vault Architecture (35%): We evaluate zero-knowledge encryption models (XChaCha20 vs AES-256-GCM), key derivation functions (Argon2id, PBKDF2 iterations), and verified third-party security audits (Cure53, Secfault, Insight Risk).
  • Autofill Reliability & Passkey Synchronisation (30%): We test automated form-filling accuracy across 100 complex login scenarios (multi-page logins, dynamic iframes, 2FA prompt detection) and native FIDO2 passkey synchronization across macOS, Windows, iOS, and Android.
  • Breach Detection & Emergency Recovery (20%): We inspect real-time dark web credential leak scanners, master password strength analyzers, and emergency access protocols without violating zero-knowledge principles.
  • Value & Commercial Transparency (15%): We weigh monthly and annual pricing against device synchronization limits, family sharing models, free tier functionality, and money-back guarantee terms.

2. Conflict of Interest Policy

Our editorial and technical testing team does not accept free subscription tiers, hardware gifts, or sponsored editorial placement fees from any password manager vendor. All testing subscriptions are procured anonymously through standard consumer channels.

3. Corrections & Verification Policy

When a vendor implements new cryptographic primitives, undergoes an updated third-party audit, or alters pricing structures, our lab re-evaluates the vault software and updates all benchmark tables promptly.
